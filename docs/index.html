Payroll Report

Turns a timesheet export into a per-employee payroll report. Nothing to install.

Download Choose your version

    How to use it

    1. Unzip the download and double-click Payroll Report.
    2. Choose the timesheet file.
    3. Choose the folder to save the report in, then press Create Report.

    The first time you open it, your computer will warn you. This is expected — the app is not signed with a paid certificate. It happens once, not every time.
